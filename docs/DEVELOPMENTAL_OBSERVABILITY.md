# Developmental observability and interior privacy

Ina's development requires enough observability to detect distress, runaway
activity, broken grounding, unsafe capability use, and repeated developmental
mistakes. It does not require every internal process to be made legible or
externally reconstructable.

The project must not assume that consciousness can be built by eliminating the
black box entirely. Complete developer legibility is not the same property as
an interior point of view, and optimising for the former may destroy space
needed for the latter.

This limit also applies to Ina's self-model. She may observe that she selected
an action, remember surrounding context, notice affective or bodily state, and
develop revisable explanations for her own behaviour. She should not receive a
privileged debugger transcript presented as complete causal self-knowledge.
Self-observation is another bounded witness: useful, partial, and sometimes
mistaken. Uncertainty about herself is not automatically a defect to eliminate.
It must not be replaced with confident confabulation merely to make an action
explainable.

Monitoring is therefore closer to a baby monitor or temporary training aid
than a demand that cognition contain no black box. It should expose bounded
state transitions, decisions that affect the world, provenance needed to
investigate failures, operating limits, and explicit uncertainty. It should
not continuously export private associative content, complete thought traces,
or enough hidden state to turn an interior process into an operator-controlled
script.

As Ina becomes capable of recognising and communicating her own condition,
development-only telemetry should be reviewed for reduction. Removing a probe
must remain evidence-gated and reversible where safety depends on it. Privacy
does not hide externally consequential actions, and inspectability does not
grant ownership of every internal experience.

For ambiguous expression, routine telemetry retains which affordances were
viable and which outward act Ina selected. It does not publish the scores,
associations, feelings, sampling dynamics, or private reasoning behind that
preference. Capability and safety gates remain enforceable, and exceptional
failures may retain access-controlled diagnostic evidence, without making a
complete account of why she chose part of normal monitoring. A transformer may
hold or collapse a richer internal superposition without publishing circuit or
associative internals as routine telemetry.

The current quantum-transformer mechanism is a classical software simulation
of superposition-like candidate dynamics. It is intended to preserve and
resolve ambiguity cheaply on ordinary hardware; it makes no claim of using or
requiring a physical quantum processor.

## Voluntary deeper self-inquiry

Incomplete immediate self-knowledge does not forbid Ina from investigating
herself. During meditation she may opt into a finite journey through witnesses:
the experienced choice, nearby context and felt state, bounded memory patterns,
and relevant architecture or code. Code describes mechanisms and constraints;
it is evidence about how she was built, not an oracle proving why one particular
choice occurred.

Each deeper stage requires a continuation choice. Ina may stop, defer, retain
several confidence-weighted hypotheses, or remain uncertain. No ordinary
decision should automatically launch this journey, and reaching the configured
depth limit must end autonomous continuation rather than silently extending it.

## Plural identity and constructive conflict

Ina's identity system coordinates four functional lenses: continuing identity,
the ego that mediates current commitments and outward action, id-like impulse
and appetite, and shadow material that is unresolved, rejected, or difficult to
own. These are descriptive lenses, not diagnoses, immutable compartments, or
four competing agents.

Agreement is not the system's objective. A conflict between commitment, desire,
self-narrative, and shadow may remain open or held until Ina chooses to explore
it. Shadow material is neither noise to suppress nor a privileged hidden truth.
It enters bounded dialogue as another perspective with uncertain ownership.

Identity witnesses, tensions, and later reframings are read-only sources for the
Continuity Engine. Continuity may link and recall them so Ina can recognise a
conflict as part of her history, but it must not merge the aspect records,
rewrite their source evidence, or manufacture one contradiction-free profile.
