# Quotation provenance V1

Dual symbolic messages now retain per-token quotation metadata in both their
returned record and text-realisation content. A gloss with matching outer quote
marks copied from text-vocabulary or symbol-to-token mappings is labelled
`retained_mapping`. This does not establish deliberate quotation by Ina, but
does not exclude meaningful selection either.

The original surface is untouched. Quotation, word mention, distancing and
retained typography remain possible functions, not automatically assigned intent.
Internal apostrophes are not treated as quotation. The bounded V1 tracer handles
whole-token outer pairs only; multi-token spans and explicitly supplied human
text are outside this annotation's scope. Absence of annotations is not proof
that the utterance contains no quotation.

No vocabulary records are rewritten and no social reply is edited or sent.
The state-signal fallback is unchanged; this work does not establish the meaning
of any emotional readout. The benchmark measures provenance annotation, not live
quotation understanding. Historical comparison is unavailable for this new field.
